# RING OF CINDERS

Original night-coliseum combat cinematic. Two fighters — **Kael** and **Sable** — trade six hits in the ash ring, then the shot holds.

This is a filmed autoplay sequence, not a fighting game. No licensed characters or brands.

## Watch

The page plays once (about half a minute, including hit-stops and one slow-motion clash), then idles on the knockout. **Replay** runs it again. **Sound on / Sound off** mutes the mix. A first tap starts audio if the browser blocks autoplay.

## Stack

Vite, TypeScript, and Three.js. Lighting, fog, and bloom are rendered in the browser. Impacts, crowd bed, and the stinger are synthesized — nothing is sampled from another soundtrack.
